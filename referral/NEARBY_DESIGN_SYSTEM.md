## 🔤 Typography

- **Primary Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Main UI)
- **Secondary Font**: Inter
- **Display Font**: Sora (Marketing & Headings)

### Font Scale
- **App Title**: 32px
- **Page Title**: 28px
- **Section Title**: 22px
- **Card Title**: 18px
- **Body**: 16px
- **Caption**: 14px
- **Small Label**: 12px

---

## 🔘 Components Specs

### Buttons
- **Height**: 56px (`h-[56px]`)
- **Radius**: 18px (`rounded-[18px]`)
- **Shadow**: Soft shadow
- **Animation**: Active press scale 98% (`active:scale-[0.98] transition-transform duration-200`)

### Cards
- **Radius**: 24px (`rounded-[24px]`)
- **Background**: White (`#FFFFFF`) / Dark (`#1E293B`)
- **Shadow**: Soft floating shadow (`shadow-[0_4px_25px_rgba(0,0,0,0.06)]`)
- **Spacing**: Large, airy padding (`p-6` or `p-8`)

### Input Fields
- **Height**: 56px (`h-[56px]`)
- **Radius**: 16px (`rounded-[16px]`)
- **Background**: `#F3F4F6` (Light gray)
- **Focus**: Emerald Green border (`#16A34A`)

### Icons
- Lucide Icons ONLY.

---

## 🗣️ Tone of Voice
- **Human-first, warm, friendly, simple, trustworthy**
- "You're all set!" (instead of "Operation Successful")
- "Nothing here yet." (instead of "No Data Available")
- "We couldn't complete that right now. Please try again." (instead of "Request Failed")
